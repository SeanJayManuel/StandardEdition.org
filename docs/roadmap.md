# Roadmap

This page lists what The Standard Edition is working on and what comes after. It isn't a complete list of everything happening in the project, and it has no dates. Plans change as we learn from real editions.

Last updated: 2026-11-15.

## Where the project is

- The site is live at [standardedition.org](https://standardedition.org), with its first edition published on 2026-11-13.
- It reads 25 outlets across left, center and right, along with federal records.
- Scheduled publishing is built but not switched on yet.

## Now

- **Publish on a schedule.** Turn on the scheduled job so an edition goes out twice a day without anyone running it.
- **Check real editions by hand.** Read a week of editions against their sources, and tune how articles are grouped into stories.
- **Cite a rating for every outlet.** Each outlet's left, center or right label should point to a published rating. Today the labels are starting assumptions.
- **Review feed terms.** Confirm each outlet's terms allow its feed to be read this way.

## Next

- **More outlets.** Grow from 25 to 100 or more, with regional outlets first.
- **Keeping ratings current.** Ratings are checked by hand today. Find a way to re-check them on a schedule that the rating services allow.
- **Outlet ownership.** Show who owns each outlet, and say when two outlets in a story share an owner.
- **Blind-spot page.** A page and a feed for stories only one side covered.
- **Installable site.** Add the site to a phone's home screen and read the last edition offline.
- **Email edition.** Under discussion, because it means storing email addresses.
- **Past editions.** A date picker for earlier days.
- **More open summaries.** Show what each summary left out, and everything that was read that day.

## Later

- **An open-source writing model.** Summaries are written with Claude today. The plan is to move to an open-source model once one is shown to write and check as well.
- **More federal records.** Court opinions, roll-call votes, nominations and agency reports.
- **State and local news.** One state and a few cities first, starting with San Mateo County, California.
- **Other countries and languages.** After US coverage is solid.

## Not planned yet

These could happen, but nobody is working on them and there's no plan for them yet.

- **Native iOS and Android apps.** An installable site comes first.
- **Sports coverage.** The edition leaves sports out for now.
- **A live feed or breaking-news alerts.** The edition is published at fixed times.
- **Reader accounts.** Settings stay in the browser today. Accounts would only be considered if a feature can't work without them.

## Out of scope

These go against what the project is for, and won't be added.

- **Ads, sponsorships or paid tiers.** The Standard Edition is free, with nothing to sell.
- **Tracking readers, or selling or renting their data.**
- **Ranking by clicks, shares or social trends.** What readers see is decided by published rules, not by engagement.
- **Original reporting or fact-check verdicts.** The site shows who reported what. It doesn't rule on what's true.

## Get involved

- To suggest something for the roadmap, [open an issue](https://github.com/SeanJayManuel/StandardEdition.org/issues/new/choose). Large features and changes to editorial rules are discussed in an issue first. See [governance](governance.md).
- To work on an item, read its entry in the [planning notes](planning.md), which has the current state and what's left to do, then follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- To propose a source or challenge a label, use the issue forms.
